ARTICLE I DEFINITIONS AND ACCOUNTING TERMS

Section 1.01 Defined Terms. As used in this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:

“Administrative Agent” means Synthetic Bank, N.A., in its capacity as administrative agent hereunder.

“Borrower” means Northwind Avionics Holdings, LLC.

“Capitalized Leases” means all leases that have been or should be, in accordance with GAAP, recorded as finance leases.

“Compliance Certificate” means a certificate substantially in the form of Exhibit D, signed by a Responsible Officer.

“Consolidated EBITDA” means, for any Test Period, Consolidated Net Income for such Test Period, plus, without duplication and to the extent deducted in arriving at Consolidated Net Income for such Test Period:

(a) Consolidated Interest Expense;

(b) provision for taxes based on income, profits or capital;

(c) depreciation and amortization expense;

(d) non-cash charges, excluding any non-cash charge that represents an accrual of or reserve for cash payments in any future period;

(e) restructuring, integration and severance costs;

(f) the amount of run-rate cost savings and synergies projected by the Borrower in good faith to be realized within 24 months after the date of the related action, net of the amount of actual benefits realized, provided that such cost savings and synergies are reasonably identifiable and factually supportable and are certified by a Responsible Officer in the Compliance Certificate;

(g) adjustments in respect of new contracts and increased pricing or volume under existing customer contracts;

(h) adjustments in respect of changes in estimates at completion on long-term contracts;

minus, without duplication and to the extent included in arriving at Consolidated Net Income, (i) non-cash gains and (ii) gains on sales of assets outside the ordinary course of business;

12

provided that the aggregate amount added back pursuant to clauses (e) and (f) for any Test Period shall not exceed 25% of Consolidated EBITDA for such Test Period (determined before giving effect to such add-backs);

provided further that the amount added back pursuant to clause (g) for any Test Period shall not exceed 10% of Consolidated EBITDA for such Test Period (determined before giving effect to such add-backs);

provided further that the amount added back pursuant to clause (h) for any Test Period shall not exceed 10% of Consolidated EBITDA for such Test Period (determined after giving effect to such add-backs).

“Consolidated Interest Expense” means interest expense of the Borrower and its Restricted Subsidiaries determined in accordance with GAAP.

“Consolidated Net Income” means the net income of the Borrower and its Restricted Subsidiaries determined on a consolidated basis in accordance with GAAP.

“Consolidated Total Debt” means, as of any date, the aggregate principal amount of Indebtedness of the Borrower and its Restricted Subsidiaries consisting of (a) borrowed money, (b) obligations under Capitalized Leases and (c) purchase money obligations, but excluding (x) undrawn letters of credit and (y) obligations under Swap Contracts.

“GAAP” means generally accepted accounting principles in the United States.

“Indebtedness” has the meaning specified in Section 7.03.

“Person” means any natural person, corporation, limited liability company, trust or other entity.

“Responsible Officer” means the chief executive officer, chief financial officer or treasurer of the Borrower.

“Restricted Subsidiary” means any Subsidiary of the Borrower other than an Unrestricted Subsidiary.

“Subsidiary” means, with respect to any Person, any entity of which a majority of the voting interests is owned by such Person.

“Swap Contract” means any interest rate, currency or commodity swap, cap or collar agreement.

“Test Period” means, as of any date, the most recently ended four consecutive fiscal quarters of the Borrower for which financial statements have been delivered.

“Total Leverage Ratio” means, as of the last day of any Test Period, the ratio of Consolidated Total Debt as of such day to Consolidated EBITDA for such Test Period.

“Unrestricted Subsidiary” means any Subsidiary designated as such by the Borrower in accordance with Section 6.14.

Section 1.02 Other Interpretive Provisions. With reference to this Agreement, unless otherwise specified, the singular includes the plural.

Section 7.11 Financial Covenant. The Borrower shall not permit the Total Leverage Ratio as of the last day of any Test Period ending on or before December 31, 2026 to exceed 4.50 to 1.00, or as of the last day of any Test Period ending thereafter to exceed 4.00 to 1.00.

Section 7.12 Fiscal Year. The Borrower shall not change its fiscal year.
